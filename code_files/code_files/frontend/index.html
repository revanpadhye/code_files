TERMS & CONDITIONS

By using our facilities, you agree to follow these terms.

  1. Bookings: Subject to availability. Late arrivals get no extra time; extensions are chargeable.
  2. Cancellations & Refunds: Contact management for applicable policies.
  3. Memberships & Passes: Valid for the stated duration; non-transferable without approval.
  4. Court Rules: Non-marking shoes only. Keep courts clean and equipment safe; no food on playing surfaces.
  5. On-Court Behaviour: Respect everyone. Abusive or disruptive behaviour is prohibited.
  1. Safety: Play responsibly and report injuries or hazards.
  2. Belongings: Secure your valuables; report lost items to management.
  3. Privacy: Customer data is used for club services. Promotional photos require consent where applicable.
  4. Facilities: Follow club instructions. Misconduct or unsafe conditions may lead to restricted access or suspended play.
